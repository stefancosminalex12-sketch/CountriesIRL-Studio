Studio terms

The short version: download only what you have the right to use, and use the Studio fairly.

Who can use it

CountriesIRL Studio is a private workspace for CountriesIRL creators and the people they invite. Access can be given or taken away at any time.

The Downloader

  • Only download videos and audio that you made, that you have permission to use, or that the law where you live otherwise allows you to use.
  • You are responsible for what you download and what you do with it. Don’t use it to re-upload other people’s work as your own.
  • YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and the other sites have their own terms, and some of them don’t allow downloading. Using the Downloader doesn’t change those terms.
  • Files pass through the Studio’s helper only to reach you, and are deleted from it within an hour. We don’t keep a list of what you download.

The Editor

The Editor runs entirely in your own browser. The clips you open and the videos you export never leave your device.

No guarantees

The Studio is provided as it is, while it is being built. Tools can change, break or go offline, especially the Downloader, which depends on sites that change often. Keep your own copies of anything you need.
